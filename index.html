How Ordering Works

Your order is prepared here on the site, then sent to BTK Coffee through Messenger for confirmation.

  1. 1

    Browse the menu

    Explore coffee, frappes, non-coffee drinks, waffles, ramyeon and fries.

  2. 2

    Add to your cart

    Choose your drinks and food, pick a size, set the quantity, then add them to your cart.

  3. 3

    Pickup or delivery

    Select how you'd like to receive your order and enter your details.

  4. 4

    Review & copy

    Check your order summary and copy it with one tap.

  5. 5

    Continue to Messenger

    Paste the summary into the BTK Coffee Messenger chat and send it.

  6. 6

    Wait for confirmation

    BTK Coffee confirms availability, the final total, and fulfillment details.

Please note

This website prepares your order — it does not process payments or send your order automatically. Operating hours, delivery coverage, delivery fees, and payment methods are confirmed directly with BTK Coffee on Messenger. Any total shown here is an estimate until BTK Coffee confirms it.

Our Story

From Late-Night Café Adventures to Your Favorite Cup

“Every cup tells a story of curiosity, creativity, and a shared love for coffee. What started as two people chasing their perfect brew became BTK Coffee — and we're excited to share that passion with you.”

BTK Coffee was born out of a shared passion for coffee between two people who simply loved exploring different cafés every night. What began as a nightly adventure to discover new flavors soon turned into a dream of crafting our own unique blend.

With countless cups and late-night experiments, we finally created an iced coffee recipe that perfectly matched our palate. After sharing it with friends and receiving overwhelmingly positive feedback, BTK Coffee was officially established.
